## Main Customer Problems
Customers are struggling to keep track of deadlines, with many explicitly requesting reminders and calendar views for assignments and exams. Performance issues, including slowness and crashing, are also a concern.

## Requested Features
The most frequently requested features are deadline reminders and calendar views for assignments and exams. Users also desire improvements in app performance and stability, along with features like dark mode, study hour tracking, a progress dashboard, and cross-device synchronization.

## Link to the Product Problem
The core product problem is that "StudyFlow users are missing deadlines. We need to improve student productivity and engagement." The customer feedback directly supports this, with a significant portion of users explicitly requesting deadline reminders and calendar features to help them manage their time and avoid missing due dates. Performance issues also indirectly impact productivity by hindering app usability.

## Top 3 Improvement Opportunities
1. **Deadline Reminders:** Implement customizable reminders for upcoming assignment and exam deadlines. This directly addresses the primary customer pain point and the stated product problem.
2. **Calendar Integration:** Enhance the calendar functionality to display all assignments and exams, allowing students to visualize their schedule and manage their time effectively.
3. **Performance Optimization:** Address the reported issues of slowness and crashing to ensure a stable and reliable user experience, which is crucial for productivity.
