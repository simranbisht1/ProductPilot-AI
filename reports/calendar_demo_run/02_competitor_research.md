## Feature Comparison

StudyFlow offers basic study tracking, while TaskMaster, EduPlanner, and FocusMate offer more comprehensive features. TaskMaster and EduPlanner include deadline reminders, calendar views, and cross-device synchronization. FocusMate offers a calendar view, dark mode, and study tracking. EduPlanner has the highest performance rating at 5/5, while StudyFlow is rated at 3/5.

## Gaps in StudyFlow

StudyFlow is missing several key features that are available in competing products:
- Deadline Reminders: Available in TaskMaster and EduPlanner.
- Calendar: Available in TaskMaster, EduPlanner, and FocusMate.
- Dark Mode: Available in TaskMaster and FocusMate.
- Cross-Device Sync: Available in TaskMaster and EduPlanner.
StudyFlow's performance rating (3/5) is also lower than the average competitor rating (4.33/5).

## Competitor Strengths

TaskMaster excels in providing deadline reminders, calendar integration, dark mode, and cross-device sync, with a strong performance rating. EduPlanner offers a complete feature set including deadline reminders, calendar, study tracking, and cross-device sync, along with the highest performance rating. FocusMate provides a good balance of features with calendar, dark mode, and study tracking, and a solid performance rating.

## Top 3 Competitive Improvements

1. **Deadline Reminders:** Implement deadline reminders to directly address the primary user need and a significant gap compared to TaskMaster and EduPlanner.
2. **Calendar Integration:** Introduce a calendar view to help users visualize and manage their assignments and exams, a feature offered by all competitors.
3. **Performance Optimization:** Improve StudyFlow's performance and stability to reach at least a 4/5 rating, bringing it closer to the performance levels of TaskMaster and FocusMate, and significantly improving user experience.
